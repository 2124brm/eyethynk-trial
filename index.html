About

Eye Thynk is a quiet corner of Instagram for people who like to pause.

Each post is two short lines and one question. No school, no guru, no rules. Just plain words about the self, time, and the one who is noticing all of it, drawn from many traditions and from everyday life.

Read one. Then look up from the screen for a moment.

On Awareness & Now

  1. The clock counts every hour. Nobody has ever counted now.
  2. You remember a thousand mornings. You have only ever woken up in one.
  3. A thought arrives and calls itself you. Watch it leave. See who is still here.
  4. Your phone holds ten thousand moments. Not one of them is this one.
  5. The tea in your hands is already cooling. Drink it before you think about it.
